# Album 68 - Scepter of Truth

A high-energy, cinematic worship record that fuses orchestral choir, stadium rock, and prophetic anthemry to proclaim Christ’s reign over deception, greed, and hidden powers. Strong prophetic and combative imagery is used metaphorically to call for truth, healing, and justice.

## Tracks

1. Break the Veil

2. Shatter the Hollow

3. Sword of Light

4. Scepter of Truth

5. Shield of Faith

6. Yeshua Arise

7. Shatter the Matrix

### Prayer

1. Break the Veil Lord, lift the veil from our eyes; let Your truth shine and expose every lie. Grant us courage to speak and live in Your light. Amen.

2. Shatter the Hollow Father, break the hollow illusions that deceive. Plant in us a vision grounded in Your Word and hope. Amen.

3. Sword of Light Holy Spirit, wield Your word within us like a sword of truth — cutting through deceit, restoring hearts to life. Amen.

4. Scepter of Truth Sovereign God, raise Your scepter over every throne of greed; bring justice, repentance, and restoration where wrong has ruled. Amen.

5. Shield of Faith Lord Jesus, be our shield in fear and trial; strengthen our faith to stand amid storms and schemes. Amen.

6. Yeshua Arise Risen King, arise in our midst; let joy and the power of Your name transform despair into praise. Amen.

7. Shatter the Matrix God of truth, break the systems that enslave; set us free by the truth that liberates and heals. Amen.
